# PantryPal interface photos

The food photos used in the home carousel and recipe cards were downloaded from Unsplash and resized/cropped for the interface.

- Vegetables: [Sharon Pittaway, Assorted Vegetables](https://unsplash.com/photos/assorted-vegetables-KUZnfk-2DSQ)
- Pasta: [Nima Naseri, Pasta Dish](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-white-plate-topped-with-a-pasta-dish-QSXPD_7_kuA)
- Curry: [CHUTTERSNAP, Chicken Thigh Curry](https://unsplash.com/es/fotos/sopa-de-muslo-de-pollo-2TSv-Z3GUtI)
- Lentil soup: [Clay Banks, Lentil Soup 1/2](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-bowl-of-soup-next-to-a-glass-of-orange-juice-CJxPligcG8s)
- Baked potato: [Markus Winkler, Baked potato with toppings](https://unsplash.com/photos/a-white-plate-topped-with-a-baked-potato-covered-in-toppings-X2X-uV6PE_A)
- Black bean tacos: [Quin Engle, Vegan zucchini and black bean tacos](https://unsplash.com/photos/vegetable-salad-on-white-ceramic-plate-hAFCfzaeVJg)
- Mushroom omelet: [Eaters Collective, Skillet](https://unsplash.com/photos/omelette-on-black-plate-6rtm6a_aVyE)

The `hero-*.png` files are wide, darkened crops of the banner photos; the `food-*` files are compact recipe card images.

`hero-overlay.png` is a project generated transparent black-to-clear gradient placed over the banner photos for text contrast.
